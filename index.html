01 — About

Hands-on lead, not a bystander

I'm a Frontend Tech Lead with more than 5 years developing and modernizing enterprise web applications. My profile is deliberately hands-on: I write the code I ask others to review, and I own the technical feasibility and the quality of what ships.

Day to day that means guiding product architecture and tooling decisions, taking part in Discovery to shape possible solutions, and growing the engineers around me through code review and high standards rather than process for its own sake.

I also write. I publish bilingual technical articles on Medium — JavaScript in Plain English and AWS in Plain English — mostly about microfrontends, team workflow and cloud fundamentals. Explaining a thing well is how I find out whether I actually understand it.

  • Frontend architecture

    Structure that survives more than one team touching it.

  • Microfrontends

    Independent deploys and real modularity — without the chaos.

  • Admin platforms

    Dense, data-heavy internal tools people use all day.

  • Auth & observability

    Knowing who did what, and what broke, before users report it.
